The AlphaGo Moment

Click to learn why it changed everything

AlphaGo's victory over Lee Sedol in 2016 was the moment that many AI researchers realized "the game is over" - a watershed event demonstrating that AI could master tasks requiring intuition and creativity once thought uniquely human.

What made this victory remarkable:

  • AlphaGo made moves that human experts initially considered mistakes but were actually brilliant long-term strategies
  • It showed that deep learning could surpass thousands of years of accumulated human knowledge
  • "Move 37" in Game 2 was so innovative that experts gave it a 1 in 10,000 chance of being played by a human

Later, AlphaZero took this further by learning purely through self-play, without human gameplay data, demonstrating a form of emergent intelligence that has profound implications for AI development across all domains.
